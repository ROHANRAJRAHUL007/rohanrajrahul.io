About

I'm an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate from NITK Surathkal, now studying for a Master of Business Laws at NLSIU Bengaluru and an M.A. in Economics at Ambedkar University Delhi.

I build full-stack and agentic AI applications, such as the Tirthofy pilgrimage super-app and LangGraph agent workflows. I want to bring that technical background to migration governance, refugee law and public policy, and contribute to evidence-based research and institutional initiatives.

Three questions organise most of what I want to work on:

  • Law: do protections work in practice? Legal frameworks for people on the move are written on paper and tested at borders and in courts.
  • Policy: what does the evidence say? Economics gives me tools to ask which policies actually help, and for whom.
  • Technology: can systems be fair and accountable? I want to see how software can support institutions without creating new risks.

Research Interests

I'm at the start of my research path. The areas below are where I plan to focus during my MBL and M.A. Economics.

  • Migration governance and refugee law. How international and national legal frameworks treat displacement, asylum and mobility.
  • Public policy and economics. Evidence-based analysis of migration policy, labour markets and welfare outcomes.
  • Technology, law and society. The role of AI and digital systems in public administration, and the legal safeguards they need.
  • Interdisciplinary research. Combining legal reasoning, economic methods and software skills in a single study.
